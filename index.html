The neighborhood

Miller Beach sits at the east end of Gary, between the steel mills and the Indiana Dunes. The house was built in 1925 during the neighborhood’s first wave of growth after its 1919 annexation by Gary.

Marquette Park’s beaches, dunes and lagoon are a short trip north, surrounded by Indiana Dunes National Park.

    Property history

    Miller Beach

    Built in 1925, during Miller Beach's first wave of residential growth after its 1919 annexation by Gary. Originally a sturdy lakeside cottage.

    Structure

    Two-story wood frame, full basement, 0.29-acre double lot (100 × 113 ft).

    Original features found

    Heart pine subfloors under laminate, plaster and lath, 1920s brass door hardware, cast-iron radiator piping.
